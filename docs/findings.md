## 1. Scale of the data
99,441 orders from 96,096 customers and 3,095 sellers between 2016-09-04 and 2018-10-17. Revenue from valid (non-canceled) orders is R$ 13.49M.

## 2. Growth
Comparing the same months (Jan to Aug), revenue in 2018 was R$ 7.34M versus R$ 3.08M in 2017, a change of +138%.

## 3. Very few customers come back
Only 3.0% of customers (2,801 of 93,358) placed more than one delivered order. The business depends mostly on winning new customers. Recommendation: a post-purchase campaign (coupon or reminder) to bring first-time buyers back.

## 4. Late deliveries hurt reviews
6.8% of delivered orders arrived after the promised date. On-time orders average 4.29 stars, while the most delayed group averages 1.70 (a drop of 2.59). Recommendation: more realistic delivery estimates and tighter carrier SLAs.

## 5. Delivery performance differs by state
AL has the highest late-delivery rate (21.4%), while PR has the lowest (4.0%). Focus carrier improvements on the worst states first.

## 6. A few categories drive revenue
The top 3 categories (health_beauty, watches_gifts, bed_bath_table) make up 25.9% of revenue.

## 7. Seller concentration
The top 10% of sellers (297 sellers) generate 67.1% of delivered-order revenue. Losing a few large sellers would hurt the marketplace, so keep them happy.

## 8. Shipping cost is heavy for some categories
Freight is 18% of product price on average across categories, and as high as 37% for 'christmas_supplies'. High shipping cost can reduce conversion.
